Company · About

About Plexa.

Plexa is an independent project building the MCP gateway for on-chain networks. We start with LayerZero's Zero and the EVM chains people already use, so any AI client can read the chain, check a transaction before it is signed and follow value as it moves between networks.

People already think out loud with AI. Every chain should be one sentence away.

01 · The idea

A nervous system for on-chain AI.

The name comes from plexus: a network of nerves where signals from many places meet and are understood together. That is the job: take the signal from every chain, every contract and every cross-chain message, and make it legible to the assistant you already talk to.

Today an assistant asked about a wallet guesses. Asked whether a transaction is safe, it can't check. We think that gap is the main thing stopping people from using AI on-chain, and it is a tooling problem, not an intelligence problem. Give the model live, verified data and a way to simulate before signing, and it becomes genuinely useful, and safe.

LayerZero's Zero is the first place this matters at scale: a new chain built for markets, launching with dozens of venues on day one. Plexa is ready for it before it exists.

02 · Principles

What we won't compromise on.

01We never hold keys

Plexa has no wallet and wants none of yours. There is nothing on our side to steal.

02Simulate before signing

Nothing should be signed that hasn't been dry-run on live state and explained in plain words.

03Honest about limits

When data is unverified, stale or missing, we say so instead of guessing.

04Plain about sources

Every answer says where it came from: the chain itself, LayerZero Scan or Sourcify.
