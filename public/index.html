What it can do

  • get_balance, get_token_info, get_block, get_transaction: live chain data
  • get_contract_info, read_contract: what a contract is and what it holds
  • simulate_transaction: dry-run before signing, with plain-language risk warnings
  • lz_track_message, lz_list_messages, lz_chain_info: LayerZero cross-chain messages and deployments
